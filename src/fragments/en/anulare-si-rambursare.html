Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Before payment

A booking request may be withdrawn without charge. Do not make any payment before BEE TRAVEL confirms availability and communicates the terms applicable to your tour.

After payment

The exact cancellation terms are communicated before payment and may depend on the tour type and third-party services already booked. Some tickets, tastings, accommodation or other partner services may have their own rules and may become non-refundable; this must be disclosed before that payment is requested.

If BEE TRAVEL cannot provide the service

If a confirmed service can no longer be provided by BEE TRAVEL, we will offer a reasonable alternative or refund amounts paid to BEE TRAVEL for the unprovided service, taking into account any non-recoverable third-party components previously accepted by the customer.

How to cancel

Email beetravel.md@gmail.com or message +373 79 520 914 as soon as possible, stating your name and booked tour/date.
