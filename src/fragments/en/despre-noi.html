About us

We travel with soul

BEE TRAVEL is about thoughtfully planned journeys through Moldova and Romania — pilgrimages, cultural tours, winery trips and tailor-made experiences.

We focus on clear itineraries, comfortable transport and careful organization so each journey feels relaxed and well structured.

We favour small groups and private tours because they offer greater flexibility and a more personal experience. For group travel, we arrange suitable transport for 8–16 passengers depending on the itinerary and availability.

We respect the places we visit and the local communities around them. Each tour clearly states what is included and what is paid separately.

People make places special — and we are here to help you discover them, with soul.
