Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This policy explains how BEE TRAVEL uses personal data submitted through beetravel.md.

Data we may collect

  • full name;
  • phone, WhatsApp and email;
  • tour, date, group size, preferred language and pickup details;
  • messages and requests you voluntarily provide;
  • technical and usage data only to the extent allowed by your cookie preferences.

Why we use data

To reply to enquiries, check availability, prepare an offer or itinerary, communicate about bookings and improve the website. We do not use our forms to collect bank-card details.

Sharing data

We may share strictly necessary information with suppliers involved in the requested service — for example transport providers, guides, accommodation providers, wineries or attractions — when needed to organize the trip. Website forms are processed through Netlify hosting infrastructure.

Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, booking, administrative requirements and handling possible complaints.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion, to the extent permitted by law, by emailing beetravel.md@gmail.com.
